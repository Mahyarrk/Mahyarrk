## 👋 Dr Mahyar Mirzazadeh, M.D.

General practitioner working at the medicine × data-science intersection
— with a sleep-medicine research background (MD thesis: AHI in REM vs
NREM sleep in obstructive sleep apnea).

**What I do here:** rebuild clinical research from its raw data, then
build tools that make that rigor automatic.

### 🔬 Featured projects

**[project-veritas](https://github.com/Mahyarrk/project-veritas)** —
a statistics auditor for published papers. It extracts every published
statistic, recomputes each one from the raw dataset, and reports which
numbers check out — with the exact computation attached. Validated on
my own thesis: both known value errors detected, zero false
verifications. *(Python · Streamlit · AGPL-3.0)*

**[osa-psg](https://github.com/Mahyarrk/osa-psg)** — reanalysis and
reproducibility audit of my own MD thesis: 99/99 published statistics
reproduced from raw polysomnography data, two genuine errors found, and
a machine-learning severity classifier built on top. The project that
motivated project-veritas. *(Python · scikit-learn · AGPL-3.0)*

### 🧭 What these projects say

Research papers are trusted far more than they are verified. My goal is
to make verification as routine as publication itself.
